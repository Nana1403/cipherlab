How to solve Caesar Cipher

A Caesar cipher shifts every letter in the alphabet by a fixed number.

Example: Shift by 3:
A → D
B → E
C → F

Convert Letters to Number, A-Z is converted to 0-25s

Example 1:
Encrypt: HELLO using shift: k=3, gives us KHOOR

Example 2:
Decrypt: KHOOR with shift 3, gives ua HElLO.

Encryption Formula:E(x) = (x+k) mod 26

Decryption Formula: D(x) = (x−k) mod 26

Brute Force Method: There are only 25 possible shifts.
Try all shifts until readable English appears.

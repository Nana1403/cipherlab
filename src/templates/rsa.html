How to solve RSA algorithm

Step 1: choose two prime numbers or use given prime numbers,
p = prime, q = prime

Step 2: finding n: n = p x q

Step 3: Find Euler’s Totient: ϕ(n) = (p−1)(q−1)

Step 4: Choose e:
Rules: greater than 1, less than ϕ(n), relatively prime to ϕ(n)

Good choices are often: 3, 5, 7, 11, 17......

Step 5: find d: ed ≡ 1 (mod ϕ(n)) which means
d=e^−1 mod ϕ(n)

Step 6: Public and Private Keys
Public Keys: (e,n)
Private Key: (d,n)

Encryption formula: C ≡ M^e (mod n)

Decryption formula: M ≡ C^d (mod n)

How to solve a Vigenère Cipher

The Vigenère cipher is like multiple Caesar ciphers combined together. Instead of using one shift number, it uses a keyword.

Main Idea: each letter of the keyword gives a different shift.
Example keyword: KEY
Shifts:
Letter Shift
K 10
E 4
Y 24
Encryption Formula: Ci=(Pi + Ki) mod 26
Where: Pi = plaintext letter
Ki = keyword letter
Ci = ciphertext letter

Decryption Formula: Pi = (Ci − Ki) mod 26
